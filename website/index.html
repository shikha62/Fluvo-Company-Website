How is Fluvo different from traditional performance agencies? +
We operate as a technical growth partner combining performance marketing, full-stack web engineering, and first-party attribution. Every engagement is led by senior growth strategists in agile 90-day sprints focused on unit economics (CAC, ROAS, LTV) with zero lock-in contracts.
What digital marketing and web services do you provide? +
We deliver full-funnel growth architecture: Technical SEO, Core Web Vitals optimization, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), paid media (Google/Meta/LinkedIn Ads), high-performance web development, marketing automation, CRM pipelines, and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO).
How do you handle attribution post-iOS 14.5? +
We implement custom first-party server-side tracking (Meta CAPI via Cloudflare/AWS, Google Tag Manager Server Container, GA4 event models) combined with blended MER and Media Mix Modeling to bypass browser signal loss. We never rely on inflated platform self-reported conversions.
How do you approach Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI search? +
We structure technical content, JSON-LD schema entity graphs, and domain citations so modern AI search systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) index and cite your brand as an authoritative entity.
How fast can we launch our first growth sprint? +
Following our initial technical audit and discovery session, we complete tracking instrumentation and CRM integration within 72 hours. Initial campaigns, optimized landing pages, and SEO enhancements go live in Days 7–10.
