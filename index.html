COMMON INQUIRIES

Everything you need to know.

Have a specific question not covered here? Schedule a call or message our team in the live chat.

How is Fluvo.in different from traditional performance agencies? +
We don't assign your account to junior account managers. Every client is partnered with a senior media buyer, creative director, and data engineer who have scaled brands beyond $100M+. Furthermore, we work in agile 90-day growth sprints with zero 12-month lock-in traps.
What is your minimum ad spend requirement? +
We typically work with enterprise and high-growth brands deploying a minimum of $30,000/month in paid acquisition budget across Meta, Google, TikTok, or programmatic channels.
How do you handle attribution post-iOS 14.5? +
We implement custom first-party server-side tracking (Meta Conversions API via Cloudflare/AWS) combined with Media Mix Modeling (MMM) and blended MER analysis. We never rely on inflated in-platform self-reported conversions.
How fast can we launch our first growth sprint? +
From initial strategy call and audit sign-off, we complete full technical account onboarding within 72 hours. Initial creative angles, landing pages, and campaign structures go live in Days 7–10.
Do you produce ad creatives or just run media buying? +
We handle the entire creative pipeline in-house: concept scripting, UGC creator recruitment, 3D motion graphics, voiceover, editing, and rapid iteration. We produce 20–35 bespoke ad variations each month.
